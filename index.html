How to read a case

  • Proven: a chain transaction, receipt or contract view shows it.
  • Reported: a provider said so. It isn't proof.
  • Inferred: the facts line up, but nothing proves the link.
  • Not visible: a confidential step only its owner can read.

What it won't do

  • It never moves funds or asks for a key. If someone offers to recover funds by DM, it's a scam.
  • It names who acts next, not who is to blame.
  • The same evidence always gives the same verdict, so anyone can check it.
